The trigger

Growth exposed a visibility problem.

Customer management was spread across Sales, Implementation and Tech Support. Data lived in a spreadsheet; HubSpot was used for Sales. No renewal process, no reliable health view, no way to prioritise attention.

What we changed

We built the CS operating layer into HubSpot.

No new platform. Three layers on the stack Rail-Flow already owned.

01

CRM & DATA FOUNDATION

Structured customer data model, renewal pipeline with stage automation, and segmentation by potential.

02

ACCOUNT OVERVIEW

All accounts at a glance — journey stage, health, latest notes, revenue and renewal date. Filterable by segment, risk or owner.

03

AI-ASSISTED INTELLIGENCE

VoC Hub explains what's happening, why, and what to look into next — pulling signals from HubSpot, Jira and usage data.

The foundation

AI needs a foundation. We built it first.

FOCUS

Decide where attention goes.

Segmentation by potential, health score, adoption and renewal stage — priorities follow evidence, not the loudest account.

OWNERSHIP

Decide who owns what, and when.

Roles across the lifecycle, clear handovers, a weekly operating rhythm, shared knowledge — quality no longer depends on one person.

DATA & TOOLS

Make the data trustworthy.

CRM standards, data quality rules and integrations of HubSpot, Jira and usage data — what the AI layer reads from.

How it reaches the team

Not just flags. The context behind them.

01

Your Week

Per CSM, their accounts only. Every critical account comes with a plain-language explanation of what is going wrong — and why it matters.

02

Friday CS Wrap-Up

What changed since last week — renewals, churns, health shifts — each with the reason behind it, not just a red or green flag.

03

VoC Hub, on every deal

Risk and expansion patterns, plus the agent's reasoning: which evidence from tickets, meetings, emails and usage led to the score.

04

Monthly: Product Theme Report

Where customers hit the product: which workflows break, which business processes get disrupted — with the affected accounts and open questions.

05

EBR deck, every 6 months

Prefilled with insights from support and sent to the CSM — the review starts from facts, not a blank slide.

06

Early Adoption Monitor

Tracks adoption after go-live — weak uptake shows up early, not at renewal.
